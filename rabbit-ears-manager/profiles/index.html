Profiles

A profile is a name, a colour and a face (an SF Symbol, or the name's initial), and whether it's a manager: one that can change the station (docs/settings-sync.md). The list is a synced setting (ProfileStore.key, through SettingsSync). Who's watching is each device's own and remembered; with several profiles and none remembered, a Who's watching? screen asks over every channel.

What a profile keeps #

Kept per profile under ProfileStore.scope(base) (the first profile, main, keeps the unscoped keys from before profiles) and synced through ProfileHistorySync (iCloud key-value, stamped, at most once a minute and when a title stops):

base key what
onDemandResume, onDemandResumeLength places in titles
onDemandRecent Recently Watched, and Continue Watching from it
onDemandList What I'm Watching
channelRecent Recently Watched Channels, for Home
radioPick their radio station; without one, the station's (radioStationID), from before
pref.<key> their own settings (ProfilePrefs): city, screensaver and how it looks, how channels change, the buffer readout, flaky warnings, the World and MBTA channels
pref.videostoreTaste the Video Store's lowest rating and earliest year (ModuleTaste); how the store looks stays the station's

Settings that are each profile's #

Agreed 2026-10-18 after going through every setting: what's a person's taste, or about where they live, is theirs; the lineup, logins, keys, what the Mac mini works out, ad breaks and On Demand's sources stay the station's. ProfilePrefs keeps the usual UserDefaults key on each device holding the value of whoever's watching there (so @AppStorage and every reader work as they did), no longer synced; switching profile puts theirs in, and a change goes to theirs. A profile without its own gets what the device had when the setting stopped being shared. A Manager pane for one of these changes the Manager's own profile's. A profile that only watches can change these, being history.

A new key that's a person's goes in ProfileStore.historyBases; that is all it takes to scope it, sync it and delete it with its profile.

Renaming #

In the Manager (On Demand › Profiles), or on the Who's watching? faces: R on the Mac, Play/Pause on the Siri Remote. The list of profiles is the station's, so a device with no manager among its profiles can't rename.

What I'm Watching #

A list saved by hand: a title's card has Add to / Remove from What I'm Watching as its last action. It shows on On Demand's home as a row after Continue Watching. It is not Continue Watching, which fills itself from what you've started (and, for a series whose last episode you finished, the next one); this is for a series between seasons, a film you mean to get to. A series saved from Continue Watching is the episode you were on (Select resumes it); saved from a shelf it's the series (Select opens its seasons).

Channels go on it too: the station menu's Add to / Remove from What I'm Watching row puts the channel playing (a group's member, when it's on the dial) on the list. It shows as a tile with the channel's name, and Ch number and what's on now; Select opens a card with Watch Channel and Remove. A channel that's off the dial isn't shown (it stays saved). The station menu has no such row on On Demand or the TV Guide.

OnDemandWatchList (OnDemandCatalog.swift) keeps the entries as they were shown, newest first, at most 60.

Home #

The station opens on Home (HomeModule, id home), a channel module on the dial like the MBTA's: the profile's own channel, titled "My Channel" or "Sam's Channel" with their avatar. Poster rows in On Demand's style, from the active profile's history, and redrawn when the profile changes:

  1. Continue Watching
  2. What I'm Watching (titles and channels; a channel's tile says LIVE and what's on now)
  3. Recently Watched Channels (RecentChannels, key channelRecent: a video channel counts after 20 seconds on it, so flipping through the dial doesn't)
  4. Recently Watched (titles)

Select on a title opens On Demand's card for it (Home doesn't have a player of its own: On Demand owns it, so Menu from the card goes to On Demand's home, and Menu there comes back here). Select on a channel tunes it. Left from the first tile and right from the last change channel, as anywhere on the dial. Launch always lands on Home (it's the dial position of HomeModule.id, not number 1, so existing numbering is left alone; new installs number it 3).

On Demand and the Video Store are apps #

Tuned to by the dial, each shows its door: the arrows still change channel, and Select goes in. Inside, the arrows are theirs and Back peels their layers (a card, a shelf, a title playing); at the top, Back steps out to the door, on the same channel, where the dial works again. On Demand opened from a Home tile skips the door, and Back at its top returns to Home. Tuning away puts you back at the door for next time. Esc on the Mac, Menu on the Siri Remote (claimed only while inside, so at the door it leaves Rabbit Ears as on any channel). OnDemandBrowser.inside, VideoStoreModel.inside, StationModel.leaveApp(). The Video Store's skipDoor setting still starts it among the shelves; Back there steps out to the door all the same, so the dial is never out of reach.

Switching #

From anywhere (any channel, On Demand, the Video Store, a title playing):

  • Apple TV: hold Select on the Siri Remote for about a second. The click that ends the hold is swallowed, so it doesn't pick a face or open the menu.
  • Mac: the P key.

A title that's playing stops when the profile changes (ProfileStore.willChange is posted while the old one is still active), so its place is saved under whoever was watching it. Hold-to-switch is untested on a real Siri Remote.

  • The quick row on On Demand's home ends in a Profile tile (when there's more than one profile): Select opens the faces, left/right pick, Select chooses.
  • The header shows who's watching; the filter drawer's Profile section and the station menu's Profile row do the same.
  • The Manager's On Demand pane adds, renames, recolours, re-faces and removes.
