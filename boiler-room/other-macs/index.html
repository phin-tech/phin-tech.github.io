Other Macs

Keep Boiler Room running on a Mac that's always on, as a Mac mini, and look at it from another, as a MacBook: its Jobs, Runs and questions, in the same window, jumping between Macs with the switcher above the Jobs, or ⇧⌘M. Each Mac still runs its own Jobs; the one shown is only the one you're looking at.

By SSH #

If ssh mini works from the MacBook, that's all it takes:

boiler-room macs add mini --ssh mini
boiler-room --on mini status

Or Settings › Other Macs › Add a Mac by SSH. Boiler Room forwards the mini's socket over SSH, with your key, and keeps the tunnel open for the window, the command line and agents alike. Nothing new listens on the mini.

By pairing #

A Mac can listen for others instead, on an address they reach, as its Tailscale address. On the mini, in Settings › Other Macs › Listen on, or:

boiler-room settings listen 100.64.1.2:7375

Then on the MacBook, Settings › Other Macs › Pair, or:

boiler-room macs pair mini 100.64.1.2:7375

Both Macs show the same six-digit code, and the mini asks you, in its window, a notification and Slaps, whether to allow it. Only then is the mini's token handed over, and kept on the MacBook in ~/.config/boiler-room/macs.toml, which only you can read. After that, each connection proves it knows the token, by HMAC of a random challenge, so the token never crosses the network again. boiler-room macs token on the mini prints it, for adding a Mac by hand with --address and --token.

What works where #

Everything the window and the command line do works on the Mac shown: Run Now, answering, schedules, Edit… (a copy's opened here, and each save sent back), and attaching to a Run, over SSH for a Run in tmux or zellij. --on mini goes before any command, boiler-room --on mini mcp gives an agent the mini's Boiler Room. The Mac shown tells you here too: each Run's news and each question it asks, as a notification titled with the Job and the Mac ("Backups · mini"), and a Loud Job's in Slaps; what was there when you switched to it isn't told again. Its own Daemon tells you on that Mac as well.
